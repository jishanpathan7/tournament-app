# SIBL Tournament Guide

**How the SurveyMonkey India Badminton League is played.** For organizers and volunteers.

---

## The ladder

Four words, four different things. Most match-day confusion comes from mixing them up.

| | What it is | How it ends |
|---|---|---|
| **Game** | One race to 15 points | First to 15, win by 2 |
| **Match** | Pair vs Pair | Best of 3 games |
| **Fixture** | **Team vs Team** | Whoever wins more matches |
| **Tournament** | All the fixtures | Top of the league wins |

Games stack into a **match**. Matches stack into a **fixture**. Fixtures decide the **league**.

> Two things people get wrong: the **game** is 15 points, not the match. And **pairs** win matches — **teams** win fixtures.

---

## Teams

**The team is the competitor.** Everything else represents the team.

- A **team** has a roster of players.
- Two players from the same team make a **pair**. A pair never crosses teams.
- Pairs play the matches. **Only teams have a league record.**

Standings show four teams, not sixty players. Player and pair numbers are statistics, not a league table.

**Odd roster?** One player plays twice so nobody sits out. Every team fields the same number of pairs.

## Matches

### Winning a game — first to 15, win by 2

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 15–12 | Game over |
| 15–14 | **Not over** — only 1 ahead, play on |
| 16–14 | Game over |
| 20–20 | Next point wins |
| 21–20 | Game over — **21 is the ceiling** |

If the score reaches 20–20, the next point ends it. No game goes past 21.

> **Tell volunteers:** "To 15. If it's close, keep going till someone's 2 ahead. If you both hit 20, next point wins."

The scoring screen says **Deuce**, **⚡ Game point**, or **⚡ Next point wins** as you tap. If it says the game isn't finished, it isn't.

### Winning a match — best of 3

First pair to win **2 games** wins the match.

| Game | Score | Winner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15–11 | Pair 1 |
| 2 | 13–15 | Pair 2 |
| 3 | 16–14 | Pair 1 |

**Pair 1 wins 2–1.** If a pair wins the first two games, stop — no third game.

### Winning a fixture — most matches

A fixture is several matches between two teams. **The team that wins more matches wins the fixture.** Points inside games don't carry over — only whole matches count.

**Team A vs Team B**, 7 matches:

| After match | Score |
|---|---|
| 3 | A 2 – 1 B |
| 5 | **A 4 – 1 B** |

Team A reaches 4 of 7 and wins. The remaining matches can't change it — whether you still play them is your call, decided before day one.

## Rankings

Every completed fixture updates the league table.

| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Fixture won | **2** |
| Fixture drawn | **1** |
| Fixture lost | **0** |

A draw only happens with an even number of matches per fixture (e.g. 3–3 of 6).

**Teams are ranked by:**

1. **Points**
2. **Matches won** — the tie-break
3. **Team name** — alphabetical, so the order stays steady

| # | Team | P | W | D | L | Matches won | Pts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Team A | 3 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 14 | **6** |
| 2 | Team B | 3 | 2 | 0 | 1 | 12 | **4** |
| 3 | Team C | 3 | 1 | 0 | 2 | 9 | **2** |
| 4 | Team D | 3 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 7 | **0** |

**Matches won** counts every match a team's pairs won — including in fixtures the team lost. Losing a fixture 4–3 still banks 3.

**Top of the league when all fixtures are done wins the tournament.**

### Top performers

A separate list. Celebrates pairs; doesn't affect who wins. Ranked by **matches won**, then matches played, then name.

> A pair with 4 wins ranks above a pair with 1 win at 100% — the list leads with wins, not percentage.

## Who does what

**Playlane handles automatically**, the moment a score is submitted: the fixture score, the league table, rankings and tie-breaks, top performers, the public page, and freeing the court. There is no recalculate button.

**You handle:**

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Before | Check-in · build teams · lock pairs · add courts · generate fixtures · schedule · publish · send scorekeeper links |
| During | **Enter every score on court, straight away** · call players · handle no-shows and injuries · watch for late courts |
| After | Check the standings · mark complete · publish results |

**The one habit that matters:** enter the score on court, before the players walk away.

## Common questions

**"Is it 15 or 21?"** The game is to 15. 21 only matters if it reaches 20–20.

**"15–14, do we stop?"** No. Play on until someone leads by 2.

**"They won the first two games — play the third?"** No. Match over at 2–0.

**"The screen won't accept my score."** Read the message under it. It says exactly why.

**"Scores are tied and the game's over."** Tap Submit game. Playlane asks who won and writes the correct score.

**"I entered a wrong score."** Tell the organizer. Never compensate in the next match.

**"A player didn't turn up / got injured."** Tell the organizer — walkover, no-show and retirement are recorded differently. Never leave a match blank.

**"Why are we second with more matches won?"** Points come first. Matches won is only the tie-break.

**"Can I use my own phone?"** Yes. The scorekeeper link needs no login and is specific to one court.

## Print this for each court

```
GAME    →  first to 15, win by 2, 21 is the ceiling
MATCH   →  best of 3 games (stop at 2–0)
FIXTURE →  Team vs Team, most matches won
LEAGUE  →  fixture win = 2 pts · draw = 1 · loss = 0

RANKED BY:  points → matches won → name

ENTER THE SCORE ON COURT, STRAIGHT AWAY.
Wrong score? Tell the organizer.
No-show or injury? Tell the organizer. Never leave it blank.
```
